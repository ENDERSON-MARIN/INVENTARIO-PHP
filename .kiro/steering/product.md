# Product Overview

This is an inventory management system built for tracking products, purchases (compras), and sales (ventas). The application manages:

- Product catalog with categories
- Supplier (proveedores) management
- Customer (clientes) management
- Purchase orders with line items
- Sales orders with line items
- Stock tracking and inventory control

The system uses CRUDBooster as an admin panel framework to provide CRUD operations for all entities. It includes user authentication, activity logging, and role-based access control through the CMS modules.

Business logic includes automatic stock updates via database triggers when purchases and sales are recorded.
